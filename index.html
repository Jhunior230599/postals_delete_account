Account Deletion — Postals

This page explains how users of Postals can request the permanent deletion of their account and associated data. We are committed to giving you full control over your personal information. Deletion is free, straightforward, and can be initiated directly from the app or by contacting our support team.

1. How to request account deletion

There are two ways to request that your Postals account be permanently deleted:

A
Delete directly from the app (recommended)
Open the Postals app and go to your Profile. Inside the profile menu, find Account Settings and tap Delete Account. Follow the on-screen confirmation steps. This is the fastest way to complete the process — no waiting required.
B
Contact us by email
If you cannot access the app or prefer to request deletion manually, send an email to jhuniorgonzalez2305@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request". Please include the username or phone number linked to your account so we can locate and process your request promptly.
C
Confirm your identity
For your security, we may ask you to verify your identity before proceeding — whether through the in-app flow or by email. This step protects you from unauthorized deletion requests on your account.
ℹ️

Before you proceed: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. If you simply want to take a break, consider logging out or disabling notifications instead.

2. What data gets deleted

Upon deletion of your Postals account — and absent any applicable legal retention requirement — the following data is permanently and immediately deleted from our systems:

Data type Description Status
Account & profile Username, display name, profile picture, bio ✓ Deleted
Photos & videos All media files you have uploaded or shared ✓ Deleted
Posts & stories All posts, captions, reactions, and story content ✓ Deleted
Messages Direct messages sent or received in the app ✓ Deleted
Comments & likes All interactions left on other users' content ✓ Deleted
Contact data Phone number and linked contact information ✓ Deleted
Usage & activity logs App usage history, device info, session data ✓ Deleted
Preferences & settings Notification settings, interests, app preferences ✓ Deleted
✅

Immediate deletion: All data listed above is removed immediately upon completion of the deletion request, provided there is no active legal hold on your account.

3. Data retention for legal purposes

In certain circumstances, we are legally required to retain some or all of your data beyond your deletion request. This occurs when:

  • A government authority, law enforcement agency, or court has issued a valid legal order requiring data preservation
  • Your account is under investigation for serious offenses such as fraud, terrorism, child exploitation, or human trafficking
  • Retention is necessary to resolve an active dispute or enforce our Terms of Service
⚠️

Legal hold: When a legal retention requirement applies, the following types of data may be preserved for as long as legally required — this includes photos, videos, messages, posts, account details, and any other content or metadata relevant to the legal matter.

Data type Retained when a legal hold applies Status
Photos & videos All media associated with the account ⚖ Retained
Messages Complete message history ⚖ Retained
Posts & content All posts, stories, and comments ⚖ Retained
Account details Profile information and registration data ⚖ Retained
Logs & metadata Activity logs, IP addresses, device data ⚖ Retained

Once the legal obligation is fulfilled, all retained data will be deleted in accordance with applicable regulations. Retained data is never used for marketing, analytics, or any other commercial purpose.

🔒

Data security during retention: Any data preserved under a legal hold is stored securely with strictly limited access. It is only shared as required by the applicable legal order.

4. Deletion timeline

Here is what happens after you submit a deletion request:

1
Request received
Your deletion request is logged immediately — whether submitted through the in-app flow or via email to our support address.
2
Identity verification (if required)
We may verify your identity before proceeding. For email-based requests this typically takes no more than 24–48 business hours.
3
Account access revoked immediately
Once deletion is confirmed, your account is deactivated, all active sessions are terminated, and your profile becomes inaccessible to other users.
4
Data permanently deleted
All your data is removed from our production servers immediately, subject to the legal retention exceptions described in Section 3 above.

5. Contact & support

If you have questions about account deletion or need assistance at any step, our team is ready to help:

  • In-app: Go to your Profile menu and tap Help & Support
  • Email: jhuniorgonzalez2305@gmail.com — include your username and a brief description of your request

We aim to respond to all email inquiries within 2 business days.
